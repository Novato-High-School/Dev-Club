You knock it back on the housing. Long, long, short.

The furrowed hum ceases histrionically. In the dimming silence you can perceive the whole building starting to settle
- and then, unexpectedly from somewhere far above, in the empty bleak corridors of a
school at night, the same three beats come back.

Something has been holding the door for you. Go up.
