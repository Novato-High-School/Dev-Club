Whatever the firewall was standing in front of, this is it.

A door. Open - by the dust on the frame, open a long time. From
here the gap reads black, not dim and not shadowed: black the
way a hole is black. What light there is in this room reaches
the threshold and stops dead, as though the dark past it were a
material rather than an absence of one.

Cold air comes up out of it from somewhere well below.

Schools have rooms with numbers on them. This is not one of
them, and nobody posts a knight on an empty corridor.
