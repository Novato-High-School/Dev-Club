"The cloud" means somebody else's computer, rented by the minute. This track
covers what that really involves: deploying an app, keeping secrets out of your
code, and reading a bill so a hobby project never turns into a surprise charge.

Microsoft offers free student accounts, and cloud skills show up on job
listings constantly.
