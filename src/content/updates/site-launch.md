title: The club has a website
date: 2026-09-09
kind: news
---

Dev Club now has a real site, built by the club and hosted for free on GitHub
Pages. Everything on it comes from files in our repository, which means adding
a project or a meeting recap is a one-file change any member can make.

If you want your name — well, your GitHub username — on it, come to a meeting
and we will walk you through your first pull request.
