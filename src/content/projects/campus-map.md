Freshmen spend the first week lost. The room numbers are not in order, the
buildings are lettered inconsistently, and a paper map does not help much when
you have four minutes to get across campus.

The idea: type a room number, get a highlighted route on a simple map that
works on a phone.

This one is unclaimed and wide open. It needs somebody who wants to figure out
how to draw and label an SVG map, which is a genuinely useful skill.

**Good first task:** claim it. Bring it up at a meeting.
